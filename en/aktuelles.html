Good to know

Questions about
what's on

When is Champagne Happy Hour at Café Reitschule?

Daily from 5 to 6:30 pm, in the bar, in the lounge or on the terrace.

What is the aperitivo at Café Reitschule?

Our Italian hour: Monday to Friday from 5 to 7 pm, aperitivo classics for €8.50, with a view of Königinstraße.

When is breakfast à la carte served?

On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm on the ground floor. No reservation is needed, as long as there is room.

How much is the Sunday brunch?

Adults pay €59, and children pay according to their age. Brunch runs every Sunday from 9:30 am to 3 pm at Moro Mou, with drinks such as prosecco, wine, beer and coffee included. Bookings are via OpenTable only; all details are on the brunch page.

Does Café Reitschule close early on some days?

Yes, on a few days for private functions. All dates and times are under Private functions, and the "Open now" notice on the website announces them a few days in advance.

How do I hear about new events?

In the events calendar on this website and through our newsletter, which comes out about once a month and can be cancelled at any time.

Does Café Reitschule celebrate New Year's Eve?

Yes, with a Champagne reception, a gala buffet and DJs on two levels. Programme, price and booking are on the New Year's Eve page.
