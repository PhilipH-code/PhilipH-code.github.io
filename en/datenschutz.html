Privacy Policy

This is a courtesy translation. The German version is legally binding.

1. Privacy at a glance

The protection of your personal data is important to us. We treat your personal data confidentially and in accordance with the statutory data protection regulations, in particular the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG), and with this privacy policy.

Without your consent, this website uses no tracking services, does not count visits and does not connect to servers of other providers. Fonts, images and all other content are hosted on our web server. We set no cookies on the public pages. Our visitor statistics only count if you consent in the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

The password-protected administration area (staff only, under /admin) uses a technically necessary session cookie (“rs_admin”) for login. It is set only in that area, contains nothing but a random session identifier and is deleted when you close the browser (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Our own visitor statistics (only with consent)

If you choose “Accept” in the cookie notice or switch on “Statistics” in the settings, we count visits with our own method on our web server. For this, a script sends a short message to our server each time a page is viewed. It contains the page viewed, the address of the page you came from (referrer) and, where present, the source tag of a link (utm_source, for example when you come from an AI assistant such as ChatGPT). We also report clicks on Book a table, Call and Email. From your browser’s identifier (user agent) we derive whether you are using a phone or a computer.

Only daily totals are stored: views per page, referring domains, device type, time (full hour) and the number of clicks. To roughly count unique visitors, we combine IP address and browser identifier with a new random key each day to form a short value. We do not store the IP address itself. We delete the key after one day; after that, the short value can no longer be linked to anyone. We set no cookies for this and share nothing with third parties. If your browser sends the “Do Not Track” signal, we do not count you even with your consent.

The legal basis is your consent (Section 25 (1) TDDDG, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Without consent, no counting takes place. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

Google Analytics 4 (not currently in use)

We do not currently use this service. Should we introduce it, we will load it only if you consent to the “Statistics” category (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, Section 25 (1) TDDDG). The following then applies: Google Analytics 4 is a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. It sets cookies (including “_ga”, stored for up to 14 months) and processes information about your use of the website. We conclude a data processing agreement with Google for this. Data may also be transferred to servers of Google LLC in the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). If you withdraw your consent, we remove any analytics cookies set. Details: Google privacy policy.

Meta Pixel (not currently in use)

We do not currently use this service either. Should we introduce it, we will load it only if you consent to the “Marketing” category (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, Section 25 (1) TDDDG). The Meta Pixel of Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland, measures the success of advertisements. Data is then transferred to Meta, possibly also to the USA (basis: EU-US Data Privacy Framework). We are jointly responsible with Meta for collecting and transmitting this data (Art. 26 GDPR). Details: Meta privacy policy.

What is stored in your browser

Some features remember settings in your browser (localStorage or sessionStorage). These entries stay on your device and are not transmitted to us. They are strictly necessary for the features you request, so no consent is required (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

  • rs-consent (localStorage): your choice in the cookie notice, with version and time. Kept until you change your choice or clear your browser data.
  • rs-still (localStorage): whether you have paused motion and marquees. Kept until you start them again.
  • rs-intro (sessionStorage): so that the opening animation runs only once per visit.
  • rs-popup-… (sessionStorage): so that a notice window appears only once per visit.
  • rs-karte (sessionStorage): which menu you opened last.

Your browser deletes sessionStorage entries as soon as you close the tab or window.

Cookie settings and withdrawal

On your first visit, a notice asks whether we may use the visitor statistics. “Accept” and “Decline” carry equal weight; as long as you do not decide, we do not count. Via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page you can view, change or withdraw your choice at any time (Art. 7 (3) GDPR): open cookie settings. We store your decision only in your browser (entry “rs-consent”, see above).

3. Data collection on this website

3.1 Server log files

When you access this website, the hosting provider automatically collects information in so-called server log files, which your browser transmits automatically. These are:

  • browser type and browser version
  • operating system used
  • referrer URL
  • host name of the accessing computer
  • time of the server request
  • IP address

Our hosting provider processes this data on our behalf (data processing under Art. 28 GDPR). This data is not merged with other data sources. It is collected on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The website operator has a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and the security of its website; for this purpose, the server log files must be recorded. The log files are deleted automatically after a short time.

3.2 Contact by email or telephone

If you contact us by email or telephone (e.g. for reservations, gift cards or event enquiries), your details, including the contact data you provide, are stored by us in order to process the enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass on this data without your consent.

Processing takes place on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, provided your enquiry is related to the performance of a contract or is necessary to carry out pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, processing is based on our legitimate interest in the effective handling of enquiries addressed to us (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required to achieve the purpose for which it was collected and no statutory retention obligations prevent deletion.

3.3 Reservations via OpenTable

For online reservations, we link to the external service OpenTable (OpenTable International Ltd.). Data processing only begins when you follow the link and enter data on the OpenTable website. The OpenTable privacy policy then applies: https://www.opentable.de/legal/privacy-policy. On this website itself, no data is transmitted to OpenTable through the link.

3.4 External links

Our website contains links to external third-party websites (e.g. Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps, partner establishments). When you access our website, no data is transmitted to these providers. Only when you click a link do you leave our offer; from that point on, the privacy provisions of the respective provider apply.

3.5 Newsletter

If you would like to subscribe to our newsletter, we need your email address. Providing your first and last name is voluntary and serves only for a personal salutation. Registration takes place using the so-called double opt-in procedure: after you sign up, we send you an email with a confirmation link. Only when you click this link do we add your address to the mailing list. This ensures that no one can sign up with someone else's address.

To prove the sign-up, we store the time of registration and of confirmation as well as the IP address used. This data serves exclusively to prove consent and to deliver the newsletter. The newsletter informs you about events, changing menus and offers of Café Reitschule.

Processing takes place on the basis of your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future by clicking the unsubscribe link at the end of every newsletter email or by sending us an informal message to contact@cafe-reitschule.de. After you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the active mailing list.

We store your data on our web server. An email service provider may be used for sending; it processes the data only on our behalf and according to our instructions (Art. 28 GDPR). We do not pass it on for any other purpose. We delete unconfirmed sign-ups after 30 days. After you unsubscribe, we delete your name immediately. We keep your email address, the times of sign-up, confirmation and unsubscription and the IP address for a further three years so that we can prove your earlier consent (Art. 6 (1) (f), Art. 17 (3) (e) GDPR). After that we delete the entry; on request, immediately.

3.6 Event inquiries via the form

For inquiries about celebrations and events we offer an inquiry form. We process the details you provide: name and email address, and optionally phone number, preferred date, number of guests, occasion, room preference and your message. We use these details solely to handle and answer your inquiry; you also receive an automatic confirmation of receipt by email.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures). Your inquiry is sent to our event team by email. Only if sending the email fails for technical reasons do we store name, email address, phone number, room preference, date and number of guests in a protected file on our web server, so that your inquiry is not lost. These entries are deleted automatically after 30 days. We delete the correspondence once it is no longer required for handling your request and no statutory retention obligations apply. It is not shared with third parties.

3.7 Protection against misuse

To prevent mass misuse of the inquiry form or the newsletter sign-up, we limit the number of submissions per internet connection. For this, we store a short value derived from your IP address with a daily changing key for one hour. We do not store the IP address itself for this purpose (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; legitimate interest: protection against spam and misuse).

4. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

If you have given consent to data processing, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR). To exercise your rights, an informal message by email to contact@cafe-reitschule.de is sufficient.

Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR

Where we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. You may object to the use of your data for email advertising at any time without giving reasons: via the unsubscribe link in the newsletter or with a short message to us.

Obligation to provide data and automated decisions

You are not obliged to provide us with any data. Without an email address, however, we cannot handle an inquiry or a newsletter sign-up. No automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR), takes place.

Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

In the event of breaches of data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent supervisory authority for us is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, www.lda.bayern.de.

5. SSL / TLS encryption

For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this site uses SSL or TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the fact that the address bar of the browser changes from “http://” to “https://” and by the lock symbol in your browser bar. When encryption is activated, the data you transmit to us cannot be read by third parties.

We point out that data transmission over the internet (e.g. when communicating by unencrypted email) can have security gaps. Complete protection of the data against access by third parties is not possible.

6. Currency of this privacy policy

This privacy policy is currently valid (as of October 2026). As our website develops or due to changed statutory or regulatory requirements, it may become necessary to adapt this privacy policy.
