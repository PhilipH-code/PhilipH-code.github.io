Christmas party in Munich

A Christmas party by the English Garden

Four rooms, a floor of its own and an event team that has hosted company parties for years. For a small team or the whole workforce.

Café Reitschule is a venue for Christmas parties in Munich-Schwabing, right by the English Garden. Teams of 10 to 20 have their own group menu. Larger groups celebrate in a room of their own: the Lounge seats 72, the Orangerie 70, the Studio on the upper floor 130 and the Restaurant 150. For large companies we combine rooms, up to the whole house. Our event team plans the food, bar, music and technology with you.

Celebrating the end of the year

An evening
for the whole team

Outside it gets dark early, inside the table lamps are lit. The bar glows in front of the palm wallpaper, and beyond the tall windows lies the English Garden. The Reitschule has stood on Königinstraße since 1927, a listed building at the edge of the park.

For years we have hosted parties here for Munich law firms, broadcasters and insurers. A Christmas dinner with your department, the agency’s end-of-year party or the company Christmas party for the whole workforce: on this evening you should be host and guest at once. Our event team takes care of everything else.

Advent in the English Garden

First to the tower,
then to the table

During Advent a Christmas market stands in the middle of the English Garden, around the Chinese Tower. From the Reitschule it is about a ten-minute walk through the park. Mulled wine at the tower, then dinner in the warm.

The Schwabing Christmas market at Münchner Freiheit, known for its arts and crafts, is a quarter of an hour away. The City of Munich publishes the dates of both markets.

For employers in Germany

What the party
may cost

A Christmas party stays tax-free for your employees as long as it costs no more than €110 per participant. The amount includes VAT and applies to up to two company parties a year.

Everything the evening costs counts: food, drinks, room, music and technology. If employees bring a partner, that person’s share is added to the employee’s. The party must be open to everyone, across the company or within a department. Only the part above €110 is subject to wage tax.

Tell us your budget per person when you enquire. Our event team will put the menu and drinks together accordingly. Your tax adviser will clarify the details.

Section 19 German Income Tax Act (in German) ↗︎

Good to know

Questions about
your Christmas party

How many people can have a Christmas party at Café Reitschule?

From ten people. For 10 to 20 guests there is a group menu of its own. The Lounge seats 72, the Orangerie 70, the Studio 130 and the Restaurant 150. For larger companies we combine rooms, up to the whole house.

Which room suits 30, 80 or 150 people?

For 30 guests the Lounge with up to 72 seats or the Orangerie with 70. For 80 guests the Studio on the upper floor with 130 seats. For 150 guests the Restaurant with 150 seats and up to 200 for a standing reception.

Can we hire Café Reitschule exclusively for a company Christmas party?

Yes, single rooms or the whole house. The Studio on the upper floor has its own entrance and its own bar. If you hire the whole house, the Restaurant closes to other guests that evening. The dates are listed under Private functions.

How much does a Christmas party at Café Reitschule cost?

That depends on the number of guests, the room, the day of the week and the menu. Tell us your budget per person and our event team will put together an offer to match.

How much may a Christmas party cost per employee to stay tax-free in Germany?

€110 per participant and party, including VAT, for up to two company parties a year (section 19 German Income Tax Act). All costs of the evening count, including room, music and the costs for partners. Only the amount above that is subject to wage tax. Your tax adviser will clarify the details.

When should we book our Christmas party?

As early as possible, ideally in September or October. December evenings in Munich are booked early, especially Thursdays and Fridays. If you are flexible, early in the week or January is easier.

What is there to eat: a served menu or a buffet?

Both, plus finger food for the reception. Our kitchen team puts the menu or buffet together for your group, with vegetarian and vegan dishes too. Let us know about allergies and intolerances while planning.

Is there music, a DJ or a band?

Yes. We arrange experienced DJs or bands for you, as well as a microphone, projector and screen for speeches and the year in review.

Can we visit the Christmas market at the Chinese Tower first?

Yes, it is about a ten-minute walk away in the middle of the English Garden. Mulled wine at the tower, then dinner with us: we plan the start of your evening with you. The City of Munich publishes the market dates.

Can a small team of ten people celebrate here?

Yes. For 10 to 20 people there is a group menu with starters, classics, salads, pasta and dessert. To book, please call +49 89 38 88 76 0.

Do you also cater Christmas parties at the office?

Yes. Our catering comes to Munich and the surrounding area, with kitchen, staff, decoration and technology, from a small circle to 1,000 guests.

Can I see the rooms beforehand?

Of course. Our event team will arrange a time and show you the rooms in person. Write to contact@cafe-reitschule.de or call +49 89 38 88 76 0.

How do guests get to the Reitschule?

Take the U-Bahn to Giselastraße (U3/U6), then it is about an eight-minute walk. Buses 54, 58, 68 and 154 stop at Thiemestraße. Parking in the streets around Königinstraße is scarce, so a taxi or the U-Bahn is more relaxed.

Are there gift vouchers as Christmas presents for employees?

Yes. Vouchers are available at the bar or by post, printed on fine paper. They are also valid at Moro Mou, our Greek restaurant in the same house.
