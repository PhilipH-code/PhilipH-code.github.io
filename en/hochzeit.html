Wedding venue in Munich

A wedding by the English Garden

Four rooms, three terraces and a listed building from 1927. For 20 to 450 guests, five minutes on foot from the registry office.

Café Reitschule is a wedding venue in Munich-Schwabing, right by the English Garden. Four rooms and three terraces hold 20 to 450 guests, from an intimate wedding dinner in the Orangerie to a celebration across the whole house. The Mandlstraße registry office is about a five-minute walk away. Our event team plans the food, bar, decoration and music with you.

A house for your day

Getting married where
Munich turns green

The Reitschule on Königinstraße has stood at the edge of the English Garden since 1927: a listed building with a balustrade, tall windows and terraces under old trees. Munich has celebrated here for generations.

For your wedding, four rooms and three terraces are available, on their own or together, up to the whole house. Over the years our event team has hosted weddings of every size, from the dinner after the civil ceremony to celebrations with several hundred guests.

Registry office and church

After the I do,
straight to the celebration

The registry office at Mandlstraße 14 is right by the English Garden, about five minutes on foot from the Reitschule. Its ceremony room holds no more than 30 people including the couple and witnesses, and a reception with catering is not possible there. We host the champagne reception and the celebration for you.

Civil ceremonies in Munich take place at the city's official wedding venues. For a church wedding, Schwabing churches such as St. Ursula on Kaiserplatz or St. Ludwig on Ludwigstraße are about a quarter of an hour away on foot.

Wedding photos

The English Garden
as a backdrop

For wedding photos, the best-known spots of the English Garden are within walking distance. Between the reception and dinner there is time for half an hour in the park with your photographer.

Private wedding photos are allowed in the English Garden without registration. Drones, driving up in the wedding car, softboxes, flash units and similar equipment are not permitted by the Bavarian Palace Administration.

Summer and winter

Outdoors in summer,
indoors in winter

From May to September the wedding belongs outside: the reception on the Königinterrasse under old trees, dinner on the Panoramaterrasse, late hours on the Sonnenterrasse.

For uncertain days there is always a plan B in the house. The Panoramaterrasse is covered and has patio heaters, and the Restaurant, Orangerie and Studio offer 180 seats indoors. So a winter wedding by the English Garden works too.

Good to know

Questions about
your wedding

How many guests fit into Café Reitschule?

From 20 to 450 guests. There are 180 seats indoors and 270 on the three terraces. The largest space is the Königinterrasse with 180 seats at tables, and indoors the Restaurant with 150.

Which room suits 50, 100 or 150 wedding guests?

The Orangerie seats up to 70 guests, the Studio on the upper floor up to 130, the Restaurant up to 150 and the covered Panoramaterrasse up to 160. For more guests we combine rooms, up to the whole house with 450 guests.

Can we hire Café Reitschule exclusively for our wedding?

Yes. You can have individual rooms or the whole house to yourselves. On such days the restaurant closes to other guests; the dates are listed under Private functions.

How far is the Mandlstraße registry office?

About 400 metres, roughly five minutes on foot. The registry office at Mandlstraße 14 is right by the English Garden. Its ceremony room holds no more than 30 people, and a reception with catering is not possible there. You celebrate the champagne reception and the party with us.

Can we have a civil wedding at Café Reitschule?

Civil ceremonies in Munich take place at the city's official wedding venues, for example at Mandlstraße 14, five minutes on foot from us. Afterwards you celebrate at Café Reitschule.

How much does a wedding at Café Reitschule cost?

It depends on the number of guests, the room, the date and the food. Our event team prepares a personal quote once you have sent us your wishes. We reply quickly.

Is there a set menu or a buffet?

Both, plus finger food for the reception. Our kitchen team puts together the menu or buffet with you, including vegetarian and vegan dishes.

Is there a DJ or band?

Yes. We organise experienced DJs or bands for you, as well as sound and presentation technology for speeches and pictures.

Do you take care of decoration and flowers?

Yes. Decoration and styling come through our partner companies, to suit your style. We have furniture for every table layout in the house.

What happens if it rains?

There is always a plan B in the house. The Panoramaterrasse is covered and has patio heaters, and indoors the Restaurant, Orangerie and Studio offer 180 seats together.

Can we take wedding photos in the English Garden?

Yes. Private wedding photos are allowed without registration. Drones, driving up in the wedding car, softboxes and flash units are not permitted by the Bavarian Palace Administration. The Monopteros and the Chinese Tower are about ten minutes on foot from us.

Can we view the rooms beforehand?

Of course. Our event team will arrange a date with you and show you the rooms personally. Write to contact@cafe-reitschule.de or call +49 89 38 88 76 0.

How early should we book?

The earlier the better, especially for Saturdays in high season. The City of Munich usually allocates registry office dates no earlier than six months in advance; you can enquire about the room for the celebration earlier.

How do guests get to the Reitschule?

By underground to Giselastraße (U3/U6), about eight minutes on foot from there. Buses 54, 58, 68 and 154 stop at Thiemestraße. Parking in the streets around Königinstraße is scarce; taxi or underground are more relaxed.

Is there a brunch the day after the wedding?

On Sundays, Moro Mou downstairs in the house serves its brunch buffet from 9:30 am to 3 pm, for €59 per person including drinks. For a larger group after your wedding, please speak to us directly.
