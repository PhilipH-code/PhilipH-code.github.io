New Year's Eve 26/27

New Year's Eve 2026/27 by the English Garden

Gala buffet, champagne and DJs on two floors. A glamorous night until four in the morning.

Café Reitschule in Munich-Schwabing celebrates New Year's Eve 2026/27 with a gala buffet: champagne reception from 7 pm, buffet from 8 pm, DJs on two floors until 4 am. The evening costs €139 per person, entry from 16 years. Reservations exclusively via OpenTable.

Good to know

Questions
about New Year's Eve

How much does New Year's Eve at Café Reitschule cost?

The evening costs €139 per person. It includes a champagne reception, canapés, the gala buffet, and DJs and dance floors on two floors.

When does the New Year's Eve evening start and end?

Doors open with a champagne reception from 7 pm, the gala buffet opens at 8 pm and the DJs start at 10 pm. The evening ends at 4 am.

How do I book for New Year's Eve?

Exclusively online via OpenTable. Unfortunately, reservations by phone or email are not possible. For questions about booking, call us at +49 89 38 88 76 0.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, from black tie to smart casual.

What is the minimum age?

Entry is from 16 years.

Can I redeem gift vouchers for New Year's Eve?

No, gift vouchers cannot be redeemed for New Year's Eve.

Are there vegan dishes at the gala buffet?

Yes. Several dishes are vegan, from the grilled vegetables and the lentil and cauliflower curry to the raspberry cheesecake. They are marked on this page.

Where does the New Year's Eve party take place?

At Café Reitschule, Königinstraße 34 in Munich-Schwabing, right by the English Garden. We celebrate on two floors. Giselastraße underground station (U3/U6) is a few minutes' walk away.
