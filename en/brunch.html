Sunday brunch by the English Garden

The big Sunday buffet at Moro Mou. Everything included, drinks too.

Café Reitschule in Munich-Schwabing serves a brunch buffet every Sunday from 9:30 am to 3 pm at Moro Mou, right by the English Garden. Adults pay €59, children €12 to €46.50 depending on age. Prosecco, wine, beer, hot drinks, juice and water are included. Reservations via OpenTable.

Sunday

One long table,
one long morning

On Sundays, Moro Mou, our Greek restaurant under the same roof, hosts the big brunch buffet. It is laid out from half past nine until three in the afternoon, drinks included.

Start sweet or savoury: freshly made eggs, oysters, Weisswurst with pretzels, cakes and pastries. From 11:30 am, hot lunch dishes join the buffet. Afterwards, the English Garden is right outside the door.

Good to know

Questions
about brunch

When is brunch at Café Reitschule?

Every Sunday from 9:30 am to 3 pm, as a buffet at Moro Mou in the Reitschule building.

How much does brunch cost?

Adults and young people aged 16 and over pay €59. Children aged 12 to 15 pay €46.50, aged 7 to 11 €29.50 and up to 6 years €12.

Are drinks included in the brunch price?

Yes. Prosecco, wine, beer, hot drinks, juice and water are included, as much as you like.

How long can I stay at brunch?

The price covers 3 hours from your reservation time.

Do I need to book brunch?

Yes. The brunch buffet can only be booked via OpenTable, around the clock. For questions, call us at +49 89 38 88 76 0 or email contact@cafe-reitschule.de.

Is there brunch on public holidays?

Yes. In addition to Sundays, we serve brunch on Christmas Day, Friday, 25 December 2026. On all other public holidays, the brunch buffet is currently not available.

Are there vegetarian and vegan dishes?

Yes. Vegetarians and vegans will find plenty of choice at the buffet.

Is brunch suitable for children?

Yes. Children pay less depending on their age: up to 6 years €12, aged 7 to 11 €29.50 and aged 12 to 15 €46.50.

What is the cancellation policy?

For no-shows or cancellations less than two days in advance, we charge €20 per person.

Where does brunch take place?

At Moro Mou, our Greek restaurant in the Reitschule building at Königinstraße 34 in Munich-Schwabing, right by the English Garden.

How do I get to brunch?

Easiest by underground: Giselastraße station (U3/U6) is a few minutes' walk away. Street parking around Königinstraße is available but limited.

Is there also breakfast à la carte at the weekend?

Yes. On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays we serve breakfast à la carte on the ground floor from 9 am to 1 pm, without reservation, while seats are available.
