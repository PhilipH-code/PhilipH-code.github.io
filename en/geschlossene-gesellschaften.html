Good to know

Questions about
special hours

Is Café Reitschule open today?

If today is not in the list above, our regular hours apply: Monday to Friday 11:30 am to 11 pm, Saturday 9 am to 11 pm, Sunday & holidays 9 am to 7 pm. The "Open now" notice on the homepage and the contact page also shows the current status.

What does "private function" mean?

From a certain time that day, the house is reserved for a private celebration, such as a wedding or a company party. Until then, you are welcome as usual.

Can I still come on such a day?

Yes, until the time stated. If you have a booking that day or would like to stay longer, it is best to call us: +49 89 38 88 76 0.

How will I know in good time?

All dates are on this page and under What's on. A few days beforehand, the "Open now" notice on the website announces them too.

Can I book Café Reitschule for my own private function?

Yes. Four rooms and three terraces hold 20 to 450 guests, individually or as the whole house. Everything is under Rooms & Celebrations; our event team will advise you at contact@cafe-reitschule.de or +49 89 38 88 76 0.
