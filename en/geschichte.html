At this site since 1863

The Story of the House

In 1863 bears lived here. Later the university rode here. Today people cook, celebrate and stay.

From 1863, the bear enclosure of Munich’s first zoo stood at Königinstraße 34. In 1927 Eugen Hönig and Carl Söldner built today’s wing, and Ludwig Maximilian University granted it the title University Riding School Munich. The kitchen has been running since that same year.

Whoever sits at Königinstraße 34 sits on Munich history. The house has lived several lives: zoo, riding school, stage for half of Schwabing. Here is what happened at this address.

The place

A house
that stays

The building

The wing at Königinstraße 34 has the air of a manor house: plain, neoclassical, with a balustrade framing the forecourt. It was built in 1927 by the architects Eugen Hönig and Carl Söldner. Today the house is a listed monument, right by the English Garden.

The name

The name came from Ludwig Maximilian University. In September 1927 it granted the house the title “University Riding School Munich” and held its riding tournaments here for decades.

The riding grounds

Riding was serious business here. A large riding hall, outdoor arenas, an outside track, and stables that held privately owned horses alongside the school horses. For almost a century, horses did their rounds on Königinstraße.

The restaurant

People have eaten and celebrated here since 1927. In the fifties the Reitschule was considered the largest establishment in Schwabing; in the early seventies Munich’s celebrities met here. Little has changed, only the hairstyles.

Today

In the summer of 2023 the riding operation’s lease came to an end, and the horses and ponies moved to Karlsfeld by the Waldschwaigsee. Since then the house belongs entirely to its hosts: downstairs Moro Mou, our Greek restaurant, everything else is Café Reitschule. And in the old riding hall, pickleball now flies across the net.

Chronicle

Eleven chapters of
Königinstraße

  1. 1863 Munich’s first zoo

    Munich’s first zoological garden opens on Königinstraße. Where the house stands today, the bears lived.

  2. Around 1900 The horses move in

    The brothers Otto and Hermann Freiherr von Faber du Faur buy the plot and set up the riding operation.

  3. 1927 Today’s house

    Eugen Hönig and Carl Söldner build the manor-like wing with the balustrade on the forecourt. In September, Ludwig Maximilian University grants the title “University Riding School Munich”. The restaurant opens the same year.

  4. 1950er All of Schwabing

    The Reitschule is considered the largest establishment in Schwabing.

  5. 1970er Celebrity haunt

    One of the most legendary houses in the city. Celebrities come and go.

  6. 1980er Long nights

    One of the wildest, too. Schwabing’s students celebrate their nights here.

  7. 1995 New hosts

    Restaurateurs Uli Springer, Klaus Pfeiffer and Matthias Scheffel take over the house and carefully bring it into the present.

  8. 2010 The terrace grows

    Major renovation. The terrace grows, the interior is renewed.

  9. 2017 More light

    New furniture, fresh colours, more light. The house takes on the open, airy character it carries to this day.

  10. 2023 The horses depart

    The riding operation’s lease ends. The horses and ponies move to Karlsfeld by the Waldschwaigsee. The riding hall and the courtyard remain.

  11. Today One house, two kitchens

    The whole house belongs to its hosts: downstairs Moro Mou cooks Greek, everything else is Café Reitschule. Pickleball is played in the old riding hall today.

About this place

What guests
often ask

Why is the house called Reitschule?

The name is a title: in September 1927, Ludwig Maximilian University granted the house the designation “University Riding School Munich” and held its riding tournaments here. The name stayed, in the restaurant too.

Are there still horses on Königinstraße?

No. In the summer of 2023 the riding operation moved with its horses and ponies to Karlsfeld by the Waldschwaigsee. The old riding hall and the wide courtyard still shape the house’s view.

What happens in the old riding hall today?

Pickleball. Instead of horses, balls fly across the net, and from the restaurant you look through the windows straight onto the courts.

Is the building a listed monument?

Yes. The neoclassical wing of 1927, designed by Eugen Hönig and Carl Söldner, is a registered monument.

How long has there been a kitchen here?

Since 1927, the year today’s building went up. In the fifties the Reitschule was Schwabing’s largest establishment; in the seventies, a celebrity haunt.

What is Moro Mou?

Our Greek restaurant downstairs, under the same roof. It belongs to the same Munich restaurant family, and gift cards from the Reitschule are valid there too.

What stood on the site of the Reitschule before?

From 1863, Munich's first zoo was here, and where the house stands today was the bear enclosure. Around 1900, the brothers Otto and Hermann Freiherr von Faber du Faur bought the plot and set up the riding school.

Who runs Café Reitschule today?

Since 1995, the Munich restaurateurs Uli Springer, Klaus Pfeiffer and Matthias Scheffel. They have carefully brought the house into the present, with major renovations in 2010 and 2017. Downstairs in the house they run the Greek restaurant Moro Mou.
