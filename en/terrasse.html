Terraces by the English Garden

Under old trees, under a roof or above the rooftops. With table service, from breakfast to dinner.

Café Reitschule at Königinstraße 34 in Munich-Schwabing has three terraces right by the English Garden, with around 270 seats outdoors. The Königinterrasse lies under old trees, the Panoramaterrasse is covered and heated, the Sonnenterrasse is on the upper floor. Open Monday to Friday 11:30 am to 11 pm, Saturday 9 am to 11 pm, Sunday & holidays 9 am to 7 pm. Dogs are welcome.

When the weather turns warm, the house moves outdoors. Three terraces surround the old university riding school, between Königinstraße and the English Garden. Service is at the table, from the first coffee to the last glass of wine.

At weekends the day starts with breakfast. At lunchtime the full menu follows, in the afternoon cakes from our own pastry chef. In the evening the string lights come on over the Panoramaterrasse.

Location

The English Garden
next door

Café Reitschule sits on the western edge of the English Garden, between the park and Leopoldstraße. From the terrace you are in the green within a few steps.

The Chinese Tower and the Monopteros are around ten minutes' walk away, the Kleinhesseloher See lake just under a kilometre.

Giselastraße underground station (U3/U6) is about eight minutes' walk. Street parking around Königinstraße is available but scarce.

Good to know

Questions about
the terrace

Café Reitschule is a restaurant, café and bar in the historic university riding school at Königinstraße 34 in Munich-Schwabing, right by the English Garden. Outdoors there are around 270 seats on three terraces, one of them covered and heated.

Does Café Reitschule have a terrace?

Yes, three of them: the Königinterrasse under old trees on Königinstraße, the covered Panoramaterrasse looking out over the English Garden, and the Sonnenterrasse on the upper floor. Together they offer around 270 seats outdoors.

Is one of the terraces covered?

Yes. The Panoramaterrasse is covered and has heaters. You can sit outdoors there in the rain and on cooler days, reliably from spring into autumn.

Do I need to book for the terrace?

You will often find a table without booking. In the evening and at weekends we recommend reserving via OpenTable; for the same day, it is best to give us a call: +49 89 38 88 76 0.

Can I have breakfast on the terrace?

Yes. On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays we serve breakfast à la carte from 9 am to 1 pm, indoors and on the terraces. No reservation needed.

Is there table service on the terrace?

Yes. Café Reitschule is a restaurant, not a beer garden: outdoors, too, we serve you at your table.

Are dogs allowed on the terrace?

Yes. Your dog is welcome, indoors and on the terraces.

When is champagne happy hour?

Daily from 5 to 6:30 pm, at the bar, in the lounge or on the terrace.

How far is it to the English Garden?

The house sits right on the western edge of the English Garden. The Chinese Tower and the Monopteros are around ten minutes' walk away, the Kleinhesseloher See lake just under a kilometre.

Can I hire a terrace for a celebration?

Yes. All three terraces can be booked exclusively: the Königinterrasse for up to 180 seated guests, the Panoramaterrasse for 160 and the Sonnenterrasse for 150. Our events team is happy to advise you at contact@cafe-reitschule.de.

How do I get to Café Reitschule?

Take the U3 or U6 to Giselastraße, then it is about eight minutes on foot. Street parking around Königinstraße is available but scarce.
