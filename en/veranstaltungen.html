Good to know

The most common
questions

How do I reserve for an event?

That depends on the organiser. As a rule you reserve online via OpenTable, around the clock. Some evenings run through their own ticket platforms. Always go by the details and the link shown with the event itself. For the same day it’s best to give us a quick call: +49 89 38 88 76 0.

Can I hire the Reitschule privately?

Yes. Four rooms and three terraces hold 20 to 450 guests. Our event team is happy to advise you at contact@cafe-reitschule.de.

Does the restaurant stay open during events?

Usually, yes. On some days the house closes early for private functions. You’ll find the current dates under What’s On.

Is there a dress code?

That depends on the event. On ordinary evenings there is none, though many guests like to dress up anyway. Some evenings such as the White Night have their own dress code. You will find it in the event details.

What happens when it rains?

Each event handles this in its own way. Usually the programme moves into the indoor rooms of the Reitschule, where 180 seats wait. Two of the three terraces are covered in any case. Check the event details to be sure.

Are children welcome?

Normally very much so. There is a dedicated children’s menu, and the Sunday brunch has its own prices for young guests. Some events carry an age limit, and a few evenings are 18 and over. Check the event details or ask the host.
