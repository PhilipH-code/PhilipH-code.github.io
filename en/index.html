Welcome

The right place,
at any hour

Between the English Garden and Leopoldstraße stands a house for nearly every occasion: weekend breakfast from 9 am, lunch from 11:30, cakes from our own pâtissier in the afternoon, cocktails at the bar in the evening. 180 seats inside, 270 outside on three terraces.

This is where Schwabing sits at one table: neighbours beside travellers, families beside walkers with and without a dog. The award-winning Wiener Schnitzel is always on the menu; on Sundays the brunch at Moro Mou joins in. Many come for an hour. Most stay longer.

Good to know

Questions and
answers

Café Reitschule is a restaurant, café and bar in the historic University Riding School at Königinstraße 34 in Munich-Schwabing, right by the English Garden. Food has been served here since 1927. The house has 450 seats across four rooms and three terraces.

Where is Café Reitschule?

At Königinstraße 34, 80802 München, in the Schwabing district. The house stands right by the English Garden, between the park and Leopoldstraße. The nearest underground station is Giselastraße (U3/U6), a few minutes on foot.

When is Café Reitschule open?

Monday to Friday 11:30 am to 11 pm, Saturday 9 am to 11 pm, Sunday & holidays 9 am to 7 pm. Kitchen open until 9:30 pm Monday to Saturday, until 6 pm on Sundays and public holidays. On a few days we close early for private functions; all dates are listed under Private functions.

How do I book a table?

Online via OpenTable, around the clock. For the same day, it is best to call us: +49 89 38 88 76 0. We recommend booking for evenings and weekends. The Sunday brunch can only be booked via OpenTable.

Does Café Reitschule serve breakfast?

Yes. On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays we serve breakfast à la carte from 9 am to 1 pm, without reservation, as long as there is room. The menu ranges from a Bavarian breakfast to shakshuka and acai bowls, with breakfast dishes from €10. On Sundays there is also the brunch buffet at Moro Mou.

How much does a meal at Café Reitschule cost?

Salads start at €16.50, pasta and risotto range from €16.50 to €19.50. The Wiener Schnitzel of veal loin is €33.50. All dishes and prices are on our menus, as text and as PDF.

Can I sit outside at Café Reitschule?

Yes, on three terraces. The Panoramaterrasse is covered and has patio heaters, with a view into the green of the English Garden. The Königinterrasse sits under old trees on Königinstraße, and there is the Sonnenterrasse on the upper floor. More about our terraces by the English Garden.

Are dogs allowed at Café Reitschule?

Yes. Your dog is very welcome, indoors and on the terraces.

Is Café Reitschule suitable for families with children?

Yes. There is a children's menu, for example kids' pasta for €9.50 or a small Wiener Schnitzel for €18.50. At the Sunday brunch, children pay less depending on their age. The English Garden is right outside the door.

Can I host a celebration at Café Reitschule?

Yes. Four rooms and three terraces hold 20 to 450 guests, on their own or combined up to the whole house. Weddings, birthdays, company and Christmas parties are celebrated here. Everything about the rooms is under Rooms & Celebrations, and our event team will advise you at contact@cafe-reitschule.de.

Is there parking at Café Reitschule?

You will find parking in the streets around Königinstraße, but spaces are limited. It is easier to come by underground (Giselastraße, U3/U6) or by bike, which you can park right outside.
