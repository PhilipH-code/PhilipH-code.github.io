Breakfast in Schwabing, Munich

Breakfast by the English Garden

Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm. No reservation needed, with a view into the green.

Café Reitschule at Königinstraße 34 in Schwabing serves breakfast à la carte on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm, right by the English Garden. No reservation is needed. The menu ranges from Munich white sausages with a pretzel for €10 to eggs Benedict, shakshuka, pancakes and acai bowls, served indoors or on three terraces.

A morning in Schwabing

Breakfast with
a view of the green

At weekends the day at Café Reitschule starts at 9 am. Until 1 pm we serve breakfast à la carte, indoors on the bright ground floor or outside on the terraces, which look into the green of the English Garden.

There is no need to book: we give out tables as long as they are free. Afterwards the park is a short walk away; the Chinese Tower is about ten minutes on foot.

After breakfast

The English Garden
on the doorstep

Café Reitschule sits on the western edge of the English Garden, between the park and Leopoldstraße. After breakfast you are in the green within a few steps.

The Chinese Tower and the Monopteros are about ten minutes on foot, the Kleinhesseloher See just under a kilometre. The Eisbach wave is about a 20-minute walk.

Giselastraße underground station (U3/U6) is about eight minutes on foot. Parking is available in the streets around Königinstraße, but spaces are scarce.

Good to know

Questions about
breakfast

When is breakfast served at Café Reitschule?

On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm, à la carte. There is no breakfast Monday to Friday; on weekdays the house opens with the main menu.

Do I need a reservation for breakfast?

No. We give out tables as long as they are free. If you want to be sure of a seat, come early.

How much does breakfast cost?

Breakfast dishes cost between €10 and €21.50. Two white sausages with a pretzel are €10, pancakes €14.50, and the classic breakfast with cheese, cold cuts and ham €20.

What is the difference between breakfast and the Sunday brunch?

Breakfast is served à la carte at Café Reitschule on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm, without reservation. The Sunday brunch is a buffet at Moro Mou downstairs, every Sunday from 9:30 am to 3 pm, for €59 per person including drinks, bookable via OpenTable only.

Is there a Bavarian breakfast with white sausage?

Yes: two Munich white sausages (Weisswurst) with a pretzel and sweet mustard for €10. Traditionally, Weisswurst is eaten before the noon bells; here it is served until 1 pm.

Can I have breakfast outside at Café Reitschule?

Yes, on three terraces looking into the green of the English Garden. The Panoramaterrasse is covered and has patio heaters, so you can sit outside on cooler mornings too.

Where can I have breakfast near the English Garden?

At Café Reitschule, right on the western edge of the English Garden at Königinstraße 34. Breakfast is served on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm. The Chinese Tower is about ten minutes on foot from here.

Are there vegetarian and vegan breakfast dishes?

Dishes without meat or fish include the sweet breakfast with Bircher muesli, avocado toast, shakshuka, the acai bowl, pancakes and French toast. Our service team will tell you whether a dish can be made vegan.

Is Champagne served at breakfast?

Yes. Moët & Chandon Imperial and Piadini Vino Spumante are available by the glass, plus sparkling wines by the bottle. There is also an alcohol-free sparkling rosé.

Is breakfast suitable for children?

Yes. Children love the pancakes with maple syrup or the French toast with vanilla ice cream, as well as eggs any style. The English Garden is right outside the door.

Are dogs allowed at breakfast?

Yes. Your dog is welcome, indoors and on the terraces.

How do I find out which allergens a dish contains?

Please ask our service team when ordering. They will tell you which of the 14 allergens subject to labelling are in a dish, such as gluten, eggs or milk.

Can I come for breakfast with a larger group?

For groups of about ten or more, please call ahead: +49 89 38 88 76 0. For a private celebration in the morning, our event team will advise you at contact@cafe-reitschule.de.

How do I get to Café Reitschule for breakfast?

The address is Königinstraße 34, 80802 München. By underground, take the U3 or U6 to Giselastraße, about eight minutes on foot from there. You can park your bike right outside; parking spaces nearby are scarce.
