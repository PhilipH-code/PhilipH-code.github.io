About the menu

What guests
ask before they come

Which menus does Café Reitschule have?

There are five menus: the main menu, the breakfast menu, a menu for groups of 10 to 20, the children's menu and the wine and Champagne list. Each one is on this page as text to read and as a PDF to download.

Until when is hot food served?

Kitchen open until 9:30 pm Monday to Saturday, until 6 pm on Sundays and public holidays. Breakfast à la carte is served on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9 am to 1 pm, after which the kitchen switches to the main menu.

How much is the Wiener Schnitzel at Café Reitschule?

The Wiener Schnitzel of veal loin is €33.50. For children, the children's menu has a smaller portion for €18.50.

Are there vegetarian and vegan dishes?

Yes. The Vegan Burger with an oat patty is explicitly vegan, at €25.50. Dishes without meat or fish include the burrata, the gratinated goat's cheese, the cauliflower couscous salad and the fresh tagliatelle with avocado pesto. Whether a dish is strictly vegetarian, for example because of the cheese, our service team will tell you.

How do I find out which allergens a dish contains?

Please ask our service team when ordering. They will tell you which of the 14 allergens subject to labelling are in a dish, such as gluten, nuts or milk.

Is there a children's menu?

Yes. It ranges from kids' pasta for €9.50 and chicken fingers to homemade pancakes for €9. Ice cream is available by the scoop.

What is there for breakfast?

The breakfast menu ranges from a Bavarian breakfast for €10 to avocado toast, shakshuka, pancakes and acai bowls. Breakfast dishes start at €10, with juices, coffee and Champagne by the glass.

Is there a menu for groups?

Yes. For 10 to 20 people there is a separate menu with starters, classics, salads, pasta and dessert. For larger celebrations, our event team will advise you at contact@cafe-reitschule.de.

Is Champagne served by the glass?

Yes, Moët & Chandon Imperial is available by the glass. The wine list also has 19 Champagnes by the bottle, from Moët & Chandon to Taittinger and Laurent-Perrier. Champagne Happy Hour runs daily from 5 to 6:30 pm.

Can I download the menu as a PDF?

Yes. Every sheet in the menu folder has an "Open as PDF" button at the bottom, so you can take any menu with you.
